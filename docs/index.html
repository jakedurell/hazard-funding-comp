What the town has not provided

Everything on this site is assembled from federal and state sources. None of it came from North Topsail Beach, because the town has not produced it.

Records requests

Public records requests to the Town of North Topsail Beach seeking its hazard mitigation grant records — applications filed, correspondence with the state, staffing and administration of the programme — have not produced responsive documents. The one item shared in response was a PowerPoint presentation.

That absence is not a gap in this analysis. It is a finding. A town that applied for funding and lost would have a file. A town that considered the question and declined would have minutes. Requests, and whatever comes back, are published in the records directory as they are made, so readers can weigh the response for themselves.

Under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 132-1 the correspondence of state agencies is a public record, which is why the state's side of this story is documented and the town's is not.

Where the data comes from

Every source is public. Nothing here requires an account, a licence, or a records request to reproduce.

FEMA — Hazard Mitigation Assistance Projects (OpenFEMA v4)
Every award, application and outcome. Programme, project type, cost share, properties proposed and properties finally mitigated, and the application status — including denied, withdrawn and pending. Downloaded whole; no filtering at source.
U.S. Census Bureau — TIGERweb Incorporated Places
Municipal boundaries for all 552 North Carolina incorporated places, used to match FEMA's free-text subrecipient field to actual towns and to draw the maps.
NC Emergency Management — Hazard Mitigation
Programme guidance, the DR-4827 notice of funding availability, and eligibility criteria. State agency correspondence quoted on these pages carries the G.S. 132-1 public records notice.
Southeastern NC Regional Hazard Mitigation Plan
The plan North Topsail Beach participates in, and the town's own published mitigation action tables — the source for its stated commitment to pursue hazard mitigation grants.
FEMA — Tropical Storm Helene (DR-4827-NC)
Declaration record establishing which counties were eligible and when.
Wake County Register of Deeds
Recorded acquisition deeds, used to verify that a FEMA buyout is traceable to a specific parcel through its open-space covenant. Methodology only; no North Topsail Beach deeds exist to examine, because no acquisition was completed.

Floodlines, by Bryan C. Johns

The analytical framework behind this site — matching federal mitigation awards to municipalities, measuring funding against risk and need, and presenting it as an interactive map — is the work of Bryan C. Johns. His project Floodlines asked whether FEMA mitigation funding tracks flood risk across Vermont's 250+ towns, and found that it follows past disasters far more closely than modelled future risk.

This site adapts his framework to a narrower question in North Carolina. The pipeline design, the index methodology and the dashboard architecture are his; the errors introduced in adapting them are ours. Floodlines is MIT licensed and its copyright notice is retained in full.
